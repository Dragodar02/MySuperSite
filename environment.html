How it was made.

The kit was designed on a 50 cm grid so every wall, floor and trim snaps cleanly; shared trim sheets keep the material count low.

Lighting uses Lumen GI with a handful of hero lights; HLODs and Nanite on rock meshes keep the scene at 60 fps.
